## Why this is needed

The website's footer links to a **Privacy Policy** and **Terms and Conditions**, but neither page
exists yet. Both links currently go nowhere.

The site's **contact form collects names and email addresses**. Under the **Data Privacy Act of
2012 (RA 10173)**, people should be told what happens to personal information they share. The site
also uses spam protection and analytics, which involve outside service providers.

**Nothing will be published until you approve it.** The pages are built but held back. The
website's automated checks refuse them while any question below is still open.

### Privacy Policy

Last updated: **[TO CONFIRM: effective date]**

DevCon Laguna is a chapter of Developers Connect (DevCon) Philippines. This policy explains what personal information this website collects, why, who else handles it, and the rights you have over it under the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**.

This website is informational. It has no user accounts, and it keeps no database of visitors. Membership and volunteer sign-ups happen on the [DevConnect Portal](https://devconnect-portal-seven.vercel.app/), which has its own privacy policy.

#### Who is responsible for your information

The personal information controller is **[TO CONFIRM: legal name of the controller: DevCon Laguna, or DevCon Philippines on the chapter's behalf]**.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **[TO CONFIRM: privacy contact, e.g. an email address the chapter controls, and the Data Protection Officer if one is designated]**.

#### What we collect, and why

**When you use the contact form**

- **Your name, email address, subject and message.** We use them only to read and reply to your enquiry.
- The website does not store them. They are sent as an email to DevCon Laguna's inbox, which is hosted by Google (Gmail). They are kept there for **[TO CONFIRM: how long enquiries are kept, and when they are deleted]**.

**To keep out spam**

- The contact form uses **Cloudflare Turnstile** to tell people apart from automated spam. To do that, Cloudflare processes technical information about your visit, such as your **IP address** and browser characteristics. Our server also sends your IP address to Cloudflare when it checks the result.
- Turnstile, as used on this site, does not set cookies.

**To understand how the site is used**

- We use **Vercel Web Analytics**. It is **cookieless**, and it doesn't identify you or follow you across other websites.
- It records which pages are viewed, the site you came from, your country, and your type of device, browser and operating system, in aggregate. It may also record which main buttons are clicked (for example “Join Us”), and that a contact message was sent. It does not record what the message says.

**When the website is delivered to you**

- Our hosting provider, **Vercel**, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and to keep it secure, as any web host does.

**Stored only on your device**

- Your choice of light or dark theme is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us.

#### Information about our officers

The “Meet Our Officers” section shows officers' names, positions and photos, managed through the DevConnect Portal. **[TO CONFIRM: how officers consent to their name and photo being published, and how they can ask for either to be removed]**.

#### Cookies

This website does not set cookies. Analytics is cookieless, spam protection runs without cookies, and your theme preference is kept in local storage on your device.

#### Who else handles your information

We use these service providers, only for the purposes above:

- **Vercel**: hosting and analytics
- **Cloudflare**: spam protection on the contact form
- **Google**: the email inbox that receives contact form messages

These providers may process information on servers outside the Philippines. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

#### Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- be informed about how your personal information is processed
- access the personal information we hold about you
- have inaccurate information corrected
- object to processing, and have your information erased or blocked
- obtain a copy of your information in a portable format
- be indemnified for damages caused by unlawful processing
- file a complaint with the **National Privacy Commission** (privacy.gov.ph)

To exercise any of these rights, use the privacy contact in “Who is responsible for your information” above. We will respond within **[TO CONFIRM: response time the chapter commits to]**.
